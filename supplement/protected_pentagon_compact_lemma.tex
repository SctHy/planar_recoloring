\section*{A compact protected-fibre certificate for pentagons}

Put $Q=\{0,1,2,3\}$.  For a boundary
$t\in(Q\cup\{\ast\})^5$ let $F_t$ be the graph of proper colourings
$x=(x_0,\ldots,x_4)$ of the pentagon satisfying
$x_i\not=t_i$ whenever $t_i\ne\ast$, with edges at Hamming distance one.
The group $\Gamma=D_5\times S_4$ acts on these graphs.  It is enough to
specify data for the $\Gamma$--orbit representatives; all labelled data
are obtained by the stated action.

The long word lists can be replaced by the following exact finite object.
A \emph{pentagon package} consists of the following records.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every nonconstant no-star orbit, and every one-star orbit, a
  cyclic word $C_t$ containing each vertex of the required component once.
  For $00000$ give its two 15-cycles.  For $00001$ and $00112$ give the
  two-row seeds and their common-port sets rather than a one-row cycle.
\item For each displayed cycle, a pool $P_t$ of edges used to join rows,
  and $K_t=E(C_t)\setminus P_t$.  For a hard seed, $K_t$ is the printed
  13-edge common-port set with its two joining edges removed.
\item A finite interface witness map.  An entry has the form
  \[
  (t,i,a;\,u,j;\,e),
  \]
  where $u$ is the normalized target boundary after changing $t_i$ to
  $a$, $j$ is the target component, and $e$ is an edge of the source and
  target components after the two restoration maps.  The required
  assertion is
  \[
  e\in K_t\quad\text{and}\quad e\in E(C_u).\tag{PF}
  \]
\end{enumerate}

A 100-line verifier is sufficient and is considerably easier to audit than
printing the words in the paper.  It enumerates all legal pentagon states,
checks that every cycle word is injective, legal, and cyclically adjacent,
checks every seed and every pool membership, and then checks (PF) for the
canonical interfaces.  It applies all $D_5\times S_4$ images and reports the
number of labelled interfaces.  The required output is
\[
  2304\text{ boundaries},\quad 23\text{ component types},\quad
  313\text{ canonical interfaces},\quad
  \min |V(F_t)\cap V_{t'}|\ge 10.
\]
The package, rather than a solver verdict, is the finite certificate: each
line is an exact equality or one-coordinate adjacency check.

\begin{lemma}[Protected-fibre gluing, compact form]\label{lem:compact-protected}
Let $A$ and $B$ be component types in the package.  Assume that every
physical copy of $A\square B$ has a Hamilton cycle that retains one copy
of each edge in $K_A$ in every $B$ row.  If two consecutive bottom layers
have boundaries differing in at most one non-star entry, and their product
components overlap, then the two reference product cycles have at least
$10$ common internal edges: one edge supplied by (PF), copied at each of
the at least ten common right states.  Suppose a component-tree vertex has
at most eight incident interfaces.  During the tree splice, before the
next child is attached, at most seven copies have been cut.  Therefore an
uncut common edge remains.  Cutting its two copies and inserting the two
actual bottom matching edges joins the cycles and leaves every protected
copy in all later rows untouched.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The witness edge in (PF) is an edge of the source protected set and of the target local
component after restoration.  At every common right state $y$ it produces
an internal edge $e\times\{y\}$ in both product cycles, and different $y$
produce different physical edges.  Thus there are at least ten common
edges.  The component tree has degree at most eight, so at most seven of
these copies can have been removed before the next child is processed.
Choose an uncut copy.  Its two copies are disjoint from all protected
edges by construction of $K_A$ and the pools.  Deleting them and adding
$(x,y)$ and $(x',y')$ in the two adjacent bottom layers is a legal
2-switch: only the bottom coordinate changes while the local pentagon
colouring is fixed.  The switch merges two circles, and induction over the
spanning tree preserves one circle and all protected copies.\qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What may be deleted immediately.}
The 811-word ordinary one-star cycles, the eight no-star cycles, the
00001 matching chain, the two 51/40/47-state paths, and the 47 individual
interface rows need not occur in the mathematical narrative.  They can be
stored in a machine-readable package and checked by the verifier.  The
main text should retain only the orbit representatives, the package
conditions, the output counts, and Lemma~\ref{lem:compact-protected}.
The six constant-source families and the selector criterion can similarly
be verifier-generated; listing their 20 selector rows is optional.

\paragraph{A genuine further deletion, conditional on a check.}
For the 19 nonconstant orbit representatives whose printed one-row cycle
is available (all except $00001,00112$ and $00000$), direct comparison of
the fixed cycles under the independent restoration maps gives at least ten
common local edges for every adjacent labelled boundary.  Hence their
pool/protected rows can be replaced by a single machine-checked
``10-edge overlap'' assertion.  This assertion is not valid for the
constant source without its component choice, and the hard types still
need their two-row/three-row seed ports.  Removing those remaining records
without a new exchange lemma would leave a quantifier gap.
