\begin{lemma}[One large-list vertex on a five-face]\label{lem:one-big}
Let $B$ be a $2$-connected plane graph of girth at least five with
$\Delta(B)\ge4$, let $x\in V(B)$, and put
\[
 H=\{v\in V(B):d_B(v)\ge4\},\quad
 s=|\{v:d_B(v)=2\}|,\quad
 D=\sum_{d_B(v)\ge3}\bigl(d_B(v)-3\bigr).
\]
Let $p$ be the number of $5$-faces of $B$ and let
$Q=\sum_{|F|\ge7}(|F|-6)\ge0$.  Suppose
\[
 2s+d_B(x)<12+Q \quad\text{when } d_B(x)\le3,
 \qquad
 2s<6+Q+d_B(x) \quad\text{when } d_B(x)\ge4 .
\]
Then some $5$-face $F$ satisfies $x\notin F$ and $|F\cap H|\le1$.
In particular this holds whenever $s=0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Bad $5$-faces are those containing $x$, together with those containing at
least two vertices of $H'=H\setminus\{x\}$.  At most $d_B(x)$ faces contain
$x$.  Let $n(F)=|F\cap H'|$ for a $5$-face $F$.  Double counting the pairs
$(v,F)$ with $v\in H'$, $v\in F$, $|F|=5$,
\[
 \sum_{|F|=5} n(F)=\sum_{v\in H'} c_B(v)\le\sum_{v\in H'}d_B(v)=3|H'|+D',
\]
where $c_B(v)$ is the number of $5$-faces through $v$ and
$D'=\sum_{v\in H'}(d_B(v)-3)$.  Since each $v\in H'$ has $d_B(v)\ge4$, we have
$|H'|\le D'$, hence $\sum n(F)\le4D'$.  Therefore at most $2D'$ bad faces
contain two or more vertices of $H'$, and the five-face identity gives
\begin{align*}
p-\#\{\text{bad }5\text{-faces}\}
&\ge 12+Q+2D-2s-d_B(x)-2D' .
\end{align*}
If $d_B(x)\le3$ then $H'=H$, $D'=D$, and the right-hand side is
$12+Q-2s-d_B(x)>0$ by hypothesis.  If $d_B(x)\ge4$ then
$D=D'+(d_B(x)-3)$ and the right-hand side is
$6+Q+d_B(x)-2s>0$ by hypothesis.
Each non-bad $5$-face either avoids $x$ and meets $H'$ in at most one vertex;
this is the required face.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
For $s=0$ both hypotheses hold: if $d_B(x)\le3$ then
$2s+d_B(x)\le3<12\le12+Q$, and if $d_B(x)\ge4$ then $2s=0<6+Q+d_B(x)$.
Thus every block of girth at least five, maximum degree at least four and
without degree-two vertices has a $5$-face avoiding any prescribed vertex and
containing at most one vertex of degree at least four.
\end{remark}
