# Task: can h(P_5) be pushed to four?

Question. The manuscript proves `4 <= h(P_5) <= 5` and resolves h = 4 for two
subclasses (at most seven distinct 5-cycles; every block subcubic), while the
general planar girth-five four-colour Hamiltonian question is left open.  This
research task asks what stands between the current proof and h(P_5) = 4.

Deliverables.

1. A literature check for any published resolution or counterexample.
2. A precise statement of the obstruction left by the manuscript's reduction.
3. One structural step that is not in the manuscript: a counting lemma that
   produces a 5-face with at most one vertex of degree at least four, avoiding
   the endblock cutvertex, whenever the block has no degree-two vertex.
4. An exact finite classification of the local C_5 list-colouring fibres for
   the boundary types that the new lemma leaves: one large-list vertex and
   four degree-three vertices.
5. An honest frontier statement: what is now reduced, what remains open, and
   what is finite evidence rather than proof.

Scope boundary.  Nothing here is adopted into the manuscript.  The counting
lemma is an analytic derivation that still needs independent review; the finite
computation only classifies local fibres and does not prove the theorem.
