\paragraph{Fixed-side source selectors (236 interfaces).}
Let $K$ be the fixed protected edge set on one side and let $B$ be the
opposite component, with pool $P_B$ and a Hamilton cycle. At a common
state $x$, put $d=\deg_B(x)$ and
$g=|(K\cap\delta_B(x))\setminus P_B|$.
If $d-g\le1$, every Hamilton cycle in $B$ has an edge in $K\setminus P_B$:
its two incident edges at $x$ cannot both lie among the other $d-g$ edges.
Either original side may supply $K$, using the prescribed first normalizer;
only the opposite side requires a Hamilton cycle.
