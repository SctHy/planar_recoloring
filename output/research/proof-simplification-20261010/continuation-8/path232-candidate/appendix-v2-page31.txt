C.2    Selected-layer identities and capacities
Order the end-, middle-, and all-distinct types as E < M < A. For distinct a, b, c, d, let
E(aab), M0 (aba), A(abc) be the images of DE , DM , DA under (0, 1, 2, 3) 7→ (a, b, c, d), and let M1 be
the reversal of M0 . Unused-colour interchange fixes E, M0 , M1 ; reversal followed by endpoint-colour
interchange fixes A. Thus E, A each have one allowed edge set and M has the two stated sets. Up
to these symmetries, the good transitions to a type no smaller are
               E(aab) : aac, abb, acb, bab, cab;   M (aba) : aca, cba;   A(abc) : abd, adc.
These exhaust one-coordinate changes; bad targets and smaller types are excluded, and unused
colours and the two ends are identified by the stated symmetries.
    In E(aab), the middle-b layer is 2P3 and the middle-c, d squares are P4 . In M0 (aba), the middle-a
layer is P8 with isolated bab, and the middle-c, d squares are P4 ; reverse for M1 . In A(abc), the
middle-a, c layers are P6 and the middle-d square is P3 with isolated bdb. These are subgraphs of
the respective selected cycles.
    Each cycle has four middle-changing edges. For E, middle colour b joins c, d twice each, with
last coordinate a twice and c, d once each. For M0 , middle colour a joins c, d twice each and every
edge has first coordinate b; reverse for M1 . For A, middle colour d joins a, c twice each, with
first coordinate in {b, c} and last coordinate in {a, b}. These paths and counts are read directly
from (C.1).
    For common endpoint lists L, R, the layer Hv is K|L\{v}| □K|R\{v}| , and there are |L \ {v, w}| |R \
{v, w}| edges between middle colours v, w. Removing the special edges gives the safe universe size
m; the original cyclic words give the retained counts x, y. The following seven families supply the
