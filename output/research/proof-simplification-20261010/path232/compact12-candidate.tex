\paragraph{Target conventions.}
For an end-change target, map $(0,1,2,3)$ to $(a,b,h_0,h_1)$,
where $a$ is repeated, $b$ is the singleton and $h_0<h_1$ are unused;
reverse the path when the singleton is at the left end.
For a middle-change target $(a,b,a)$, use this colour map; columns
$0,1$ mean without and with path reversal. The two unused-colour
orders give the same edge set.
For an all-distinct target $(a,b,c)$ with unused colour $d$, use
$(a,b,c,d)$. The other orientation is the map $(c,b,a,d)$ followed
by path reversal, and gives the same edge set.
