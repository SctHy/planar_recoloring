For the two constant-boundary components $C_0,C_1$, use the ordered pools
\[
\begin{split}
O_0={}&(12123\!-13123,\ 12312\!-12313,\ 21231\!-31231,
          \ 23121\!-23131,\ 31212\!-31312),\\
O_1={}&(12132\!-13132,\ 13212\!-13213,\ 21213\!-21313,
          \ 21321\!-31321,\ 32121\!-32131).
\end{split}
\]
Their underlying sets are rotation orbits of five edges in the printed
$15$-cycles. Put $K_i=E(C_i)\setminus O_i$. Thus every $K_i$ is invariant
under $\rho_r(x)_k=x_{k+r\pmod5}$. The first two pool edges are used to
join two or three copies of $C_i$ along a $P_2$ or $P_3$; every physical
row retains $K_i$ and the last three pool edges. Those three edges suffice
for the factor-path tree whenever its added physical load is at most three.
Other component types may retain their original pools and seeds.

\begin{proof}[Constant-source protected interfaces]
Let $K_H$ be the unchanged protected set of the hard type $00001$.
The following four edges belong to $K_H$, by their occurrence in the
printed common-edge set and exclusion from its pool:
\[
a=31212\!-31232,\qquad b=12132\!-32132,\qquad
c=21313\!-21323,\qquad d=13123\!-23123.
\]
Changing the constant boundary at position $j$ from $0$ to a colour
$q\in\{1,2,3\}$ produces a connected target of type $00001$.
Let $p_q$ be the lexicographically first colour permutation with
$p_q(0)=0$ and $p_q(q)=1$. Explicitly, its images on $(0,1,2,3)$ are
$(0,1,2,3)$, $(0,2,1,3)$ and $(0,2,3,1)$ for $q=1,2,3$.

Any map selected by the manuscript's normalization convention must
have this permutation and one of the two coordinate maps
\[
 N_{j,\epsilon}(x)_k=p_q(x_{j-4\epsilon+\epsilon k}),
                    \qquad \epsilon\in\{+1,-1\}.
\]
Indeed, the four repeated boundary entries must map to $0$, the unique
changed entry to $1$, and the latter must occupy target position $4$.
For either coordinate direction the first admissible colour permutation
is $p_q$; the convention merely decides which direction is encountered first.

The table gives an edge $e_{i,q}\in K_i$ and its two normalized images
at $j=0$. All six source memberships follow directly from the printed
$15$-cycles and the two pools above.
\[
\begin{array}{cc|c|cc}
i&q&e_{i,q}&N_{0,+1}(e_{i,q})&N_{0,-1}(e_{i,q})\\ \hline
0&1&23121\!-23123&a&b\\
0&2&31231\!-31232&c&d\\
0&3&21231\!-23231&d&c\\
1&1&21213\!-23213&b&a\\
1&2&31321\!-32321&d&c\\
1&3&21321\!-21323&c&d
\end{array}
\]
For an arbitrary position $j$, take $e=\rho_{-j}(e_{i,q})$.
Rotation invariance gives $e\in K_i$, and for either allowed direction
\[
 N_{j,\epsilon}(\rho_{-j}(e_{i,q}))
                 =N_{0,\epsilon}(e_{i,q})\in K_H.
\]
Consequently $e$ belongs to the actual restored target protected set.
The six parameter families cover all thirty constant-source interfaces,
without assuming that the complete canonical target protected sets
are rotation-equivariant.
\end{proof}
