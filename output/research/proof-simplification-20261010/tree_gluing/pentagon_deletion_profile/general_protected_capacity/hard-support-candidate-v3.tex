% Fixed-support mechanism, not a new all-C5 theorem.
\paragraph{Fixed hard support and its missing-edge budget.}
For either displayed hard protected set $K_t$ ($t=00001,00112$),
put $J_t=G[V(K_t)]$ and $M_t=E(J_t)\setminus K_t$.
Coordinate comparison of the twenty displayed endpoint words gives
$|E(J_t)|=17$, $|K_t|=11$, and the following six missing edges:
\[
\begin{aligned}
M_{00001}={}&\{13132\!-
13232,13230\!-23230\}\cup
\{x3120\!-x3123,x3210\!-x3230:x=1,2\},\\
M_{00112}={}&\{13020\!-13030,21020\!-21030,21230\!-23230,
21301\!-31301,31301\!-32301,32320\!-32321\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus their coordinate budgets are $(1,0,1,2,2)$ and $(1,2,0,2,1)$.
In particular $J_{00001}$ is saturated by $K_{00001}$ in coordinate
$1$, and $J_{00112}$ by $K_{00112}$ in coordinate $2$.
These are fixed full-support properties, independently of an interface.

For an adjacent boundary $u$, delete from $J_t$ the vertices illegal
for $u$, obtaining $J_t(u)$. Let $\delta_j(u)$ count the surviving
edges of $M_t$ in coordinate $j$. If an opposite protected basis $L$
retains $\ell_j$ edges of $J_t(u)$, then
\[
 |K_t\cap L\cap E_j(J_t(u))|\geq\ell_j-\delta_j(u).
\]
Indeed every retained edge outside $M_t$ already belongs to $K_t$.
These retention counts equal the opposite-side counts previously
computed on $W=V(K_t)\cap V(L)$: an edge of $L$ inside $J_t(u)$
has both endpoints in $V(K_t)$ and in $V(L)$, hence in $W$;
conversely $W$ lies in $V(K_t)$ and is legal for $u$.
No Hamilton-cycle hypothesis is needed. The fixed saturated coordinate
therefore succeeds as soon as $L$ retains one port there. Under the
target normalizer its physical coordinate is $s+\varepsilon j$ modulo $5$.
The weaker missing-edge criterion allows other coordinates and removal
of missing edges made illegal by $u$.

For the two exceptional hard self interfaces
$00001\xrightarrow{4\to3}00001$ and
$00112\xrightarrow{0\to2}00112$, the prescribed restored endpoint
supports meet in six vertices. Their induced graphs are respectively
$P_2+4K_1$ and $P_3+3K_1$; each side retains every edge of this
joint support, giving one and two common protected edges by capacity.

\emph{Scope.} The fixed saturated-coordinate criterion has finite
coverage $32/53$ among the nonconstant-source hard-incidence interfaces;
the missing-edge criterion has coverage $51/53$, with the two self
interfaces just stated. The other-side retention inequalities for those
$51$ interfaces are currently finite selected-basis evidence, not a
symbolic classification or a theorem for arbitrary selected cycles.
Dynamic saturation on $W=V(K)\cap V(L)$ is a different condition.
