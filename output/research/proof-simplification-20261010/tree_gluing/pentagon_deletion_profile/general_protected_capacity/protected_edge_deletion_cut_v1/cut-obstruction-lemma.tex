Let $K$ be a fixed protected edge set on one side and $B$ the complete
component on the other side, with a Hamilton cycle and reserve pool $P_B$.
Put $S=(K\cap E(B))\setminus P_B$ and $H=B-S$. If $H$ has no Hamilton
cycle, every Hamilton cycle of $B$ contains an edge of $S$. In particular,
this holds if $H$ has a vertex of degree at most one, is disconnected,
or has an articulation vertex. A spanning cycle on at least three vertices
has minimum degree two, is connected, and remains connected after deleting
any one vertex. Since $S$ is also outside the fixed-$K$ side's reserve
pool, such an edge is a protected common edge. Only the Hamilton cycle
on the $B$ side is quantified; the other side's $K$ is fixed.
