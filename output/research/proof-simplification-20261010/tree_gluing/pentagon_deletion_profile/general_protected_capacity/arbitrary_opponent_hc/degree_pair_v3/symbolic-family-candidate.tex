\paragraph{A local degree bound for hard-source interfaces.}
For a proper word $v$, let
$B_i(v)=Q\setminus\{v_{i-1},v_i,v_{i+1}\}$, with indices modulo five.
If $v$ is legal for boundary $u$, its degree is
$d_u(v)=\sum_i|B_i(v)\setminus\{u_i\}|$.
Let $g_u(v)$ count its incident edges of the source protected set
$K_A$ that avoid the prescribed restored target pool.
Every Hamilton cycle of the target component containing $v$ uses at least
$\max\{0,2+g_u(v)-d_u(v)\}$ such edges: at most
$d_u(v)-g_u(v)$ incident edges are outside this set, and a cycle uses two.
Thus a positive bound gives an edge of $K_A$ in the restored $K_B$.

The following three words suffice. Their alternatives and their source
protected neighbours follow from the printed words and pools:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|l}
t&v&(B_0,B_1,B_2,B_3,B_4)&K_A\text{ neighbours}\\\hline
00001&32132&(\{0,1\},\{0\},\{0\},\{0\},\{0,1\})&12132,32130\\
00112&13023&(\{0,2\},\{2\},\{1\},\{1\},\{0\})&12023,13020\\
00112&13230&(\{2\},\{0\},\{0,1\},\{1\},\{2\})&13030,23230
\end{array}
\]
For $u=t[j\leftarrow q]$, the degree formula and the prescribed
restored pools give the positive bounds in the following table.
All these target types are ordinary and have a single component.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|l|c}
t&v&(j,q)&(d_u,g_u)\\\hline
00001&32132&(0,1)&(2,1)\\
00001&32132&(0,2),(1,1),(2,2),(2,3),(3,2)&(3,2)\\
00112&13230&(0,3)&(2,2)\\

00112&13230&(2,0)&(2,1)\\
00112&13023&(4,0)&(2,1)
\end{array}
\]
For the additional transition $00001\to00000$, the word $32132$
lies in restored constant component $1$ and has $(d_u,g_u)=(2,1)$.
The other constant component is not covered by this argument.
Each specified target vertex has the displayed degree; no regularity
of the target graph is asserted. Simultaneous coordinate and colour
images of the source, target and specified pools preserve the argument.

Only these thirteen ordinary interfaces and the one constant-component
interface are supplied by this bound. The fixed target-pool memberships
are still premises read from the literal pools. No selected target cycle,
retention value or arbitrary choice of pool is required. In particular,
hard target seeds are not treated as whole-component Hamilton cycles.
