\paragraph{A designated-port transport criterion.}
Let $K_t$ be the displayed hard protected set and let $g$ be a
prescribed coordinate/colour map from an adjacent boundary $u$ to
the opposite representative $s$. If a legal port $e_g$ belongs to
$K_t$ and $g(e_g)$ belongs to the displayed opposite basis outside
its pool, then $e_g\in K_t\cap g^{-1}(K_s)$.
The choice $e_g$ may depend on the map. Only these named memberships
are required; neither the basis nor its pool need be equivariant.

\paragraph{One port for nine interfaces.}
For $t=00112$ put $e=13230\!-23230$, which is a hard protected port.
Write $h_a(w)_j=(12)(w_{(a-j)\bmod5})$ and let $p$ be the colour
permutation $1\mapsto2\mapsto3\mapsto1$, fixing $0$.
The three adjacent-boundary families have exactly these prescribed
opposite maps:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 u& s&g\\\hline
00012&00012&\mathrm{id},h_2\\
00312&00123&p,h_1\\
00102&00102&\mathrm{id},h_1
\end{array}
\]
Each row represents three recorded interfaces. Their needed images are
\[
h_2(e)=13103\!-13203,\qquad
h_1(e)=31031\!-32031,\qquad
p(e)=21310\!-31310.
\]
The displayed $00012$ cycle protects $e,h_2(e)$, the $00123$ cycle
protects $p(e),h_1(e)$, and the $00102$ cycle protects $e,h_1(e)$.
The six memberships are read directly from those cycles and their
pools. Thus the designated-port criterion proves all nine interfaces
using the same hard-coordinate port $e$.

\paragraph{Two ports for a four-map family.}
For $t=00112$, $u=00110$, put
$A=21301\!-21321$ and $B=32301\!-32321$; both are hard protected.
Let $\rho(w)_j=w_{(4+j)\bmod5}$,
$\eta(w)_j=w_{(1-j)\bmod5}$ and let $\sigma=(23)$ act on colours.
The four prescribed maps to $00011$ are
$\rho,\sigma\rho,\eta,\sigma\eta$.
Choose $A$ for the two rotations and $B$ for the two reflections.
Their images are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 &\mathrm{id}&\sigma\\\hline
\rho(A)&12130\!-12132&13120\!-13123\\
\eta(B)&23103\!-23123&32102\!-32132
\end{array}
\]
All four are displayed protected ports of the $00011$ cycle.
The criterion therefore proves the five recorded interfaces in this
family. The four transported local retained subsets have empty common
intersection, so the direction-dependent choice is essential for a
single designated-port proof of this family.

\emph{Scope and size.} These two arguments cover fourteen distinct
recorded hard-incidence interfaces. They quantify over precisely the
displayed maps and selected bases, not arbitrary normalizers or cycles.
The broader saved-list classification supplies two fixed ports covering
thirteen and twelve distinct interfaces, respectively, but requires
ten and nine image memberships across a longer parameter list.
It is retained separately as finite evidence. Neither that classification
nor the two arguments above has established a shorter replacement for
the main fifty-three-entry certificate.
