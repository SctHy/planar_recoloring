\paragraph{Aggregating low-degree deficits.}
Let $G_s,G_t$ be the two full fibre components, with vertex sets $V_s,V_t$,
and let $C,D$ be arbitrary Hamilton cycles of those components.
Fix edge pools $P,Q$, and put $U=V_s\cap V_t$, $O=V_s\setminus U$.
All recolouring edges on common endpoints coincide in the two graphs.
For $S\subseteq U$ whose target degrees satisfy $d_t(v)\le3$, put
\[
 A=\sum_{v\in S}(4-d_t(v)),\quad
 B=\min\{2|O|,|E_{G_s}(S,O)|\}
   +\sum_{v\in S}d_{(P\cup Q)\cap E(G_s[U])}(v).
\]
Then $C\setminus P$ and $D\setminus Q$ have at least
\[
 \max\{0,\lceil(A-B)/2\rceil\}
\]
common edges.

Indeed let $\ell(v)$ count the two $C$ incidences at $v$ lost either
to an endpoint outside $V_t$ or to $P\cup Q$ within $U$.
At most $\min\{2|O|,|E_{G_s}(S,O)|\}$ incidences of the first kind
occur, and the second kind is bounded by the displayed pool-degree sum;
therefore $\sum_{v\in S}\ell(v)\le B$.
There are $2-\ell(v)$ safe $C$ edges at $v$ in $G_t$.
Since $D$ uses two of the $d_t(v)$ incident edges, it contains at least
$(4-d_t(v))-\ell(v)$ of these safe edges. The nonnegative actual
number of common safe incidences is at least this quantity even when
it is negative. Summing gives at least $A-B$ common incidences;
each common edge contributes at most two, proving the bound.

This is a conditional graph-and-pool statement independent of the
two selected cycle edge sets. Positive coverage on fixed literal
fibre inputs remains finite evidence. Hard $D$-port components do
not supply whole-component Hamilton cycles and are excluded here.
