# Retained transport and forward local-degree union

The v5 transport selection covers thirty distinct interfaces of the fifty-three nonconstant-source hard-incidence interfaces. The old forward one-sided local rule covers nineteen source-hard interfaces with an opposite ordinary Hamilton-cycle component. Their exact overlap is eleven, their union is thirty-eight, and fifteen remain. The eight cases added by the local rule alone are preserved in the result JSON.

| Role | Total | Union covered | Remaining |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hard source, ordinary target | 26 | 21 | 5 |
| Ordinary source, hard target | 22 | 16 | 6 |
| Hard on both sides | 5 | 1 | 4 |

The transport proof uses the frozen selected opposite basis and pool for each prescribed map. The local source-hard proof uses the stated hard protected edge set and a graph-degree condition that applies to any opposite Hamilton cycle with the stated pool. The eleven common cases can be justified by the latter broader premise without selected opposite-cycle memberships. This case-set extraction does not rerun or independently review either mathematical calculation.

The old forward implementation excludes hard targets. That execution scope does not establish a mathematical obstruction to switching the two interface roles when the ordinary source has a whole Hamilton cycle and the transported hard target supplies the fixed protected edge set. No reversed-role calculation is performed here. Only the five hard-hard interfaces lack a whole-cycle side. The new bidirectional study is a separate scope; its later counts must not be attributed to the old nineteen-case result.

All complete case keys and disjoint lists are in `transport-degree-union-results-v1.json`, produced by `compare_transport_degree_union_v1.py` from retained JSON files only. Frozen v4/v5 are unchanged. No graph, membership or 313-interface producer is rerun and no manuscript is edited. The standard record validator checks schema, paths and hashes only.
