% General set-capacity lemma; independent of cycles or Hamiltonicity.
\paragraph{Protected edge capacity.}
Let $A,B$ be two edge bases in graphs with common legal edge universe
$H$, and let $P\subseteq A$, $Q\subseteq B$ be their pools. For one
coordinate class $H_j\subseteq E(H)$ put
\[
\begin{gathered}
a=|A\cap H_j|,\quad b=|B\cap H_j|,\quad m=|H_j|,\\
F=(P\cup Q)\cap H_j,\quad f=|F|,\quad
r_A=|A\cap F|,\quad r_B=|B\cap F|.
\end{gathered}
\]
Then the two protected sets $K=A\setminus P$, $L=B\setminus Q$
have at least
\[
 a+b-m-r_A-r_B+f
\]
common edges in $H_j$. Indeed, $A\cap(H_j\setminus F)$ and
$B\cap(H_j\setminus F)$ have sizes $a-r_A$ and $b-r_B$ and lie in
a universe of size $m-f$. Their intersection is exactly
$K\cap L\cap H_j$, so inclusion--exclusion proves the bound.
Using $r_A,r_B\le f$ gives the weaker bound $a+b-m-f$.
No basis is required to be a cycle or to cover all states.
