First suppose both boundaries have the middle-change type. Write
$t=(a,b,a)$ and $s=(a,c,a)$. They can differ only in their middle
entry, so $a,b,c$ are distinct. Let $d$ be the fourth colour and let
$Q_d=\{(u,d,w):u,w\in\{b,c\}\}$ be the square of $B_{aaa}$
with middle colour $d$. This square lies in both fibres. By the
square-support property, each allowed cycle retains three of its
four edges. Their intersection therefore contains at least two
edges of $Q_d$. Both are safe, since $P_t=P_s=\varnothing$.

For the remaining interfaces, order types as $E<M<A$ and orient
the transition so its source has no larger type. Normalize its
selected source cycle as before. There are eight remaining targets
from $001$, four from $010$, and three from $012$. The displayed
source stabilizers reduce them to five, two, and two target orbits.
Only the target $101$ has two allowed cycle edge sets. The resulting
nine rows contain ten edge-pair entries and exhaust all cases not
already covered by the square argument.
