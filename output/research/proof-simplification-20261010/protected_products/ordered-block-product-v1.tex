\begin{lemma}[Protected products along an ordered block factor]
Suppose that $V(B)$ is partitioned into paths $P_1,\ldots,P_m$ of order two
or three, and for each $i<m$ an actual edge of $B$ joins $P_i$ to $P_{i+1}$.
Let $K\subseteq E(A)$ and let $e,f$ be distinct edges of $E(A)\setminus K$.
If $A\square P_2$ and $A\square P_3$ have Hamilton cycles retaining every
edge of $K\cup\{e,f\}$ in every physical path row, then $A\square B$ has
a Hamilton cycle retaining every edge of $K$ in every physical $B$ row.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the specified product cycle on each block. At the interface between
$P_i$ and $P_{i+1}$, use $e$ for odd $i$ and $f$ for even $i$:
delete its two copies in the joined rows and add the corresponding two
cross edges. Each block has at most two interfaces, which use distinct
reserve edges. Thus every copy required for a later join is still present,
even if the two interfaces use the same physical row. Processing the
blocks in order merges their disjoint cycles into one. No edge of $K$
is deleted.
\end{proof}
