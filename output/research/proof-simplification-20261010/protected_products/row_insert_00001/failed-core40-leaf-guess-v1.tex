\paragraph{A failed leaf-count shortcut.}
Deleting $12312,12313,13212,13213$ from the $00001$ component leaves
a forty-vertex graph with sixty-four edges. Its degree distribution is
ten vertices of degree two, sixteen of degree three, ten of degree four,
and four of degree five. In particular, it has no leaves.
For example, the vertices left beside a removed pair retain edges to
the twenty-four-state core through recolouring their fifth coordinate.
Consequently the tentative assertion that four new leaves force at
least four $XY$ cuts is false; no lower bound $t\ge4$ or six-cut
lower bound follows from it. The valid count obstruction remains
$r+q=4$, forcing both $YY$ edges into the cut matching and the new
row forest.
