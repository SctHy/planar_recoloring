\paragraph{A minimal auxiliary-set repair for the $00001$ component.}
Write $K_0$ for the original twelve-edge auxiliary set and put
\[
 h=12313\,12323,\qquad h'=13120\,23120,\qquad
 K_*=(K_0\setminus\{h\})\cup\{h'\}.
\]
This keeps twelve auxiliary edges and removes every auxiliary edge
incident with $12312,12313,13212,13213$, the four endpoints forced
by any one-row matching insertion. It also retains the four canonical
target edges used by all six constant-source families:
\[
31212\,31232,\quad12132\,32132,\quad
21313\,21323,\quad13123\,23123.
\]
Exactly three of the old individual witnesses require replacement:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{source}&j&\text{new excluded colour}&\text{new witness}\\
\hline
00001&1&3&21313\,21323\\
00001&4&2&13120\,23120\\
00001&4&3&12310\,32310
\end{array}
\]
For the first row, the prescribed target normalizer maps its two
words to $21231,23231$, which are an auxiliary edge of $00102$.
For the second row the colour transposition $(1\,2)$ exchanges
$h'$ and $13210\,23210$, both in $K_*$.
For the third row the inverse target colour map
$(1,2,3)\mapsto(3,1,2)$ sends $h'$ to $12310\,32310$.
The remaining 280 individual witnesses and all thirty images of
the six constant-source families retain their old membership.
These statements are literal finite checks against the frozen
interface input, not the construction of replacement protected seeds.

The old reserve $13210\,13212$ must be excluded. Any pair from the
other six old reserves is disjoint from $K_*$ and makes
$D_*=K_*\cup R$ a fourteen-edge linear forest. In the forty-state
graph obtained by removing the four forced endpoints, the necessary
propagation rules ``a protected degree-two vertex admits no other
edge'' and ``an available degree-one vertex must use its only edge''
produce neither an isolated vertex nor a protected cycle for all
fifteen pairs. Thus the proposed repair passes these necessary
checks. A protected two-row cycle and a compatible new-row forest
have not yet been constructed.
