\paragraph{The fixed two-row seed cannot be extended by matching insertion.}
Partition the $00001$ component into $X$ of order twenty and $Y$ of
order twenty-four using the established sign $\chi$. The set $X$ is
independent, and $Y$ induces exactly the two disjoint edges
$12312\,12313$ and $13212\,13213$.
Every internal edge in either row of the printed two-row seed crosses
this partition. Hence a matching $M$ cut from one row has equally many
endpoints in $X$ and $Y$.
If the added row is a spanning nonisolated linear forest with endpoint
set $V(M)$, the difference between its $Y$ and $X$ degree sums is eight.
This difference equals twice the number of its edges inside $Y$,
requiring four such edges when only two exist. Thus this insertion
is impossible, even without edge protection.
