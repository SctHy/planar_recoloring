                 Finite certificates for Hamiltonian thresholds
                          in planar recolouring graphs
                                                Abstract
          This supplement contains the literal local certificates used in Hamiltonian and Hamilton-
      connected thresholds for planar recolouring graphs. The supplied Python verifiers read these
      words directly and check the complete finite case coverage. The inductions and arbitrary-order
      restoration arguments are proved in the article.

The files verify_adjacent_pairs.py, verify_path232.py, and verify_pentagon.py use exact
integer and finite-set operations and require only the Python standard library. Run python3
verify_all.py in this directory to check all certificates and rejection of corrupted data. See
README.md for the input contract and build instructions.


A     Adjacent-pair endpoint certificates
These certificates use the graph F (A, B) and the type (|A|, |B|, |A ∩ B|) defined before the adjacent-
pair lemma in the article. The two-digit word ab denotes (a, b). Each displayed word lists all states
once, with consecutive states differing in one coordinate; the 333 word is cyclic. The counts in
Table A.1 include all colour permutations preserving the listed pair of lists, coordinate exchange
when it preserves the type, and path reversal. The verifier also computes all feasible endpoint pairs
by exact subset dynamic programming, including the excluded pairs.

B.2    Selected-layer identities and capacities
Order the end-, middle-, and all-distinct types as E < M < A. For distinct a, b, c, d, let
E(aab), M0 (aba), A(abc) be the images of DE , DM , DA under (0, 1, 2, 3) 7→ (a, b, c, d), and let M1 be
the reversal of M0 . Unused-colour interchange fixes E, M0 , M1 ; reversal followed by endpoint-colour
interchange fixes A. Thus E, A each have one allowed edge set and M has the two stated sets. Up
to these symmetries, the good transitions to a type no smaller are
              E(aab) : aac, abb, acb, bab, cab;   M (aba) : aca, cba;   A(abc) : abd, adc.
These exhaust one-coordinate changes; bad targets and smaller types are excluded, and unused
colours and the two ends are identified by the stated symmetries.
    In E(aab), the middle-b layer is 2P3 and the middle-c, d squares are P4 . In M0 (aba), the middle-a
layer is P8 with isolated bab, and the middle-c, d squares are P4 ; reverse for M1 . In A(abc), the
middle-a, c layers are P6 and the middle-d square is P3 with isolated bdb. These are subgraphs of
the respective selected cycles.
    Each cycle has four middle-changing edges. For E, middle colour b joins c, d twice each, with
last coordinate a twice and c, d once each. For M0 , middle colour a joins c, d twice each and every
edge has first coordinate b; reverse for M1 . For A, middle colour d joins a, c twice each, with
first coordinate in {b, c} and last coordinate in {a, b}. These paths and counts are read directly
from (B.1).
    For common endpoint lists L, R and distinct allowed middle colours v, w, the layer Hv is
K|L\{v}| □K|R\{v}| , and there are |L\{v, w}| |R\{v, w}| edges between middle colours v, w. Removing
the special edges gives the safe universe size m; the original cyclic words give the retained counts
x, y. The following seven families supply the capacities used in the proof:
                                  t→s     blocks      (m, x, y)

                                aab → abb   Hc         (2, 2, 2)
                                aab → acb   H∗         (2, 2, 2)
                                aab → cab Hc , Hd (3, 2, 2), (1, 1, 1)

Three-row insertion rule. Let C2 be a Hamilton cycle of A□P2 , with row digits 0, 1. Suppose
its second row contains three disjoint edges ui vi , and the states of A partition into three paths
with respective endpoints ui , vi . Replace each cut edge (1, ui )(1, vi ) by its path in row 2 and the
cross edges at both endpoints. These paths are internally disjoint and cover the new row, so they
subdivide three edges of C2 into one Hamilton cycle of A□P3 . If DA occurs in both old rows and in
the new paths, and avoids the three cut edges, it survives in every row of the derived seed.
