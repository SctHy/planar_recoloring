Finite certificates for Hamiltonian thresholds in planar recolouring graphs Abstract This supplement contains the literal local certificates used in Hamiltonian and Hamilton- connected thresholds for planar recolouring graphs. The supplied Python verifiers read these words directly and check the complete finite case coverage. The inductions and arbitrary-order restoration arguments are proved in the article. Run python3 verify_all.py to check the certificates and rejection of corrupted data. The exact verifiers require only the Python standard library; README.md gives their input contract and build commands. Let C 2 be a Hamilton cycle of A□P 2 , with row digits 0, 1. Suppose its second row contains three disjoint edges u i v i , and the states of A partition into three paths with respective endpoints u i , v i . Replace each cut edge (1, u i )(1, v i ) by its path in row 2 and the cross edges at both endpoints. These paths are internally disjoint and cover the new row, so they subdivide three edges of C 2 into one Hamilton cycle of A□P 3 . If D A occurs in both old rows and in the new paths, and avoids the three cut edges, it survives in every row of the derived seed.
