\begin{abstract}
This supplement contains the literal local certificates used in
\emph{Hamiltonian and Hamilton-connected thresholds for planar recolouring graphs}.
The accompanying verification programs reconstruct the finite local graphs and
check the legality, coverage, adjacency, symmetry classes, and required
interface and protected-edge conditions of the supplied certificates. The
inductions and arbitrary-order restoration arguments are proved in the article.
\end{abstract}
